# Synk Features

## Accounts

- Sign up with personal details, interests and a unique username
- Email verification with resend
- Log in with email or username
- Forgot password and change password
- Edit profile, photo, username and email
- Delete account and all its data

## Activities

- Create activities with a title, description, photo, place, date, time and participant limit
- Public or private activities
- Join with a six-digit code; private activities send a join request the host accepts or rejects
- Waitlist with automatic promotion and your place in line
- Status tags: Available, One More Needed, Full
- Scheduling conflicts are blocked
- Hosts can edit, delete and share their activities
- Upcoming and past activities, swipe to delete
- Activities and friends are limited to users of the same gender

## Discover

- Search in Arabic and English that handles typos
- Filters by time, place and category
- Live map with activity markers
- Top Picks based on your interests and location
- Weather for the activity day and place
- See which friends are going

## People

- View other profiles with recent activities and ratings
- Friends: send, accept, reject, cancel and remove requests
- Suggested people
- Rate participants after an activity
- Block and report users

## Chat

- A group chat for every activity, closed after it ends
- One-to-one chats with friends
- Send photos, locations and activities
- Chat starters, mute, delete chats and delete messages for everyone

## Notifications

- Push notifications on Android
- ntfy or Telegram on iPhone
- Notification center
- Turn each type on or off

## More

- Weekly streak on Home
- Arabic and English with RTL
- Contact support from Settings, with replies by email
- Web admin dashboard for stats, support requests and reports
