Synk is an app for meeting new people by doing things together. Tell it what you're into, from padel and hiking to board games and coffee, and it shows you plans happening near you. Join one in a tap or post your own, then chat with the group before you meet up.

How Synk works

01

Find your missing piece

Open Synk, pick Arabic or English, and you're in.

02

Find your thing

Padel tonight, a board game night this weekend, a pottery class next week. Pick what you're into and Synk shows you what fits.

03

Show up, or start your own

Join a plan in one tap, or post your own and let people come to you.

04

See what's around you

Open the map and see what's happening near you.

05

Talk before you meet

Every activity gets a group chat, so you're not walking in as a stranger.

06

Make it stick

Add the people you liked, collect badges as you go, and plan the next one together.
